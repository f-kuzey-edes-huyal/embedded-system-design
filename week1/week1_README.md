## Experiment: Blink LED (500 ms)

In this experiment, the LED turns on for 500 milliseconds (0.5 seconds) and then turns off for 500 milliseconds (0.5 seconds) repeatedly.

### Components
- 1 × LED
- 1 × 220Ω resistor
- Jumper wires
- Breadboard
